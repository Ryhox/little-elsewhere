Privacy

Little Elsewhere · last changed 12 October 2026

Little Elsewhere is a free hobby game made by Ryhox. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not use cookies.

What is saved, and where

Your progress and your settings are saved in your browser's local storage, on your own device. They are not sent to me or to anyone else.

You can copy your save out and bring it back with a save code (Options, then Export or Import). You can erase it with Options, then Start over, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

What the web host receives

The game is a set of plain files. To send them to you, the company that hosts this page receives your IP address and the usual technical details of your browser, as with any website, and may keep them in its logs for a while. I do not receive, look at or use that data.

Once it has loaded, the game makes no connections to any other service.

Questions

You can reach me through GitHub, or open an issue on the game's page there.
